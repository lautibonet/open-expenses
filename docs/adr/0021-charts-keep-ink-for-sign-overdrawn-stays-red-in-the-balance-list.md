# Charts keep ink for sign; the balance list keeps red for overdrawn

The Stats graphs render every sign in On Surface ink: the year overview's Net columns rise above or hang below the zero line in ink, and the balance strip draws overdrawn months as ink fills descending from the line. The Negative-Stays-Ink rule in DESIGN.md previously listed "an overdrawn account balance" among the red warnings while also stating that negative figures render in ink whatever the page — the two clauses conflicted about the strip's overdrawn months.

Ruling: the shipped chart behavior is intentional and the doc yields. Inside a chart, a sign is a shape the reader compares against the zero line — a minus is a fact, not an alarm — so ink carries every sign there, and painting overdrawn months red would both alarm on ordinary data and fuse with the red already reserved for expense columns in the overview. Red keeps the overdrawn account balance in the balance list (the Stats screen's per-account rows), where it is a warning about money owed rather than a data point in a series, alongside the other sanctioned warnings (error strips, destructive confirmations).

DESIGN.md's Negative-Stays-Ink rule is amended to state this split: charts keep ink at every sign; the balance list keeps red for the overdrawn balance; red's warning list names the balance-list exception explicitly.
